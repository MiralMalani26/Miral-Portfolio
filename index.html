About me

I design user experiences and interfaces for digital products, and I also create logos, social media posts and print designs in Illustrator and Photoshop.

I like making things that look clear, feel easy to use, and fit the brand behind them. Working across both UI/UX and visual design helps me keep layouts, typography and colour consistent from the first sketch to the final screen.
