Fixora

Two apps, one service: a customer app to buy, book and track appliance care, and an expert app to manage jobs.

Role
UI/UX designer (solo)
Tools
Figma, FigJam
Deliverable
Customer + expert apps
Open Figma prototype ↗

Problem

Customers struggle to find reliable appliance service, and skilled technicians lack a steady way to receive and manage jobs. Fixora connects both sides: customers can buy, book and track services, and experts can manage orders and update job status.

Goal

Make buying, booking and managing appliance care clear for customers, and job handling simple for experts.

My thinking

Two users, two apps

Customers and experts have different needs, so each gets its own focused app.

Colour with purpose

Blue for the customer app: calm and trustworthy. Orange for the expert app: action.

Simple checkout

A 3-step stepper (address, confirm, payment) and Basic, Standard and Premium plans.

Clear expert actions

“Update status” and “Start service” keep job handling obvious.

User flows

Customer app

Drag, scroll or tap a screen to enlarge.

Expert app

Drag, scroll or tap a screen to enlarge.

What I would improve

Tracking & ratings

Live service tracking and ratings for customers.

Earnings & schedule

An earnings view and schedule for experts.

Edge states

Empty and error states across both apps.

Real users

Test with real users and refine.
